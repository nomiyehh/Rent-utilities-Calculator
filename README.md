# 🏠 Rent & Utility Bill Calculator

A lightweight, practical calculator designed to split rent, electricity units, food expenses, and household utilities evenly or proportionally among flatmates and roommates.

---

## 📌 Features

- **Base Rent Splitting:** Divide total monthly flat rent evenly or based on room occupancy.
- **Utility & Electric Bill Tracking:** Calculate electricity charges based on total units consumed multiplied by per-unit rates.
- **Shared Expenses:** Include common bills such as Wi-Fi, water, gas, or shared groceries.
- **Clean Output:** Generates an itemized breakdown of the total amount each person owes.

---
